# Contributing to MHGURecomp

All sorts of contributions are welcome, from anyone. See a bug that needs fixing? A localization mistake? A new feature that might be useful? Feel free to submit it!

However, please do not submit pull requests that make it easier to "extract NSP files" or decrypt ROMs in any fashion.
These will be categorically rejected and closed. MHGURecomp is only playable by bringing your own copy's `exefs` and `romfs` files, and this will not change.
